# 求职漫画插画

本组图片使用内置图片生成工具创作，用于首页五个求职阶段。未复制参考网站的插画。原始生成文件保留在生成目录，项目使用本目录的副本。图片是求职场景插画，不代表尚未上线产品的实际界面或能力。

## 文件与最终生成提示词

Offer 场景在初次生成后进行了局部解剖修正，最终采用修正版。修正提示词：

Correct the anatomy of this original pencil manga illustration. The character currently has an extra third arm/hand under their chin. REMOVE the hand touching the chin and its entire associated central forearm/sleeve. Replace that small area with the normal uninterrupted white sweatshirt chest/neck. Keep exactly TWO arms and TWO hands total: the left-side hand holding the left document and the right-side hand holding the right document. Preserve both documents, both remaining hands, face, glasses, hair, expression, scale, table, composition, graphite textures, background and black-and-white palette. No other changes.
