# 校园探索插画

使用内置图片生成工具创作，延续网站的黑白铅笔漫画视觉。三张图片为课程自学、项目竞赛、科研升学的方向插画，不代表已经上线的产品功能。项目使用本目录 PNG 副本，原始生成图片保留。

## 最终提示词

### campus-study.png

Use case: illustration-story. Create an original black-and-white graphite pencil manga illustration for a university learning and growth website. Style: refined editorial Japanese manga, delicate pencil grain, fine crosshatching, crisp expressive ink-like outlines, warm relatable college students, natural anatomy, no color, no 3D. Main recurring character is a young adult with round black glasses, tousled short dark hair and a plain white sweatshirt. Landscape 3:2 canvas. Airy white background, sparse campus setting, subjects and props entirely within frame, generous negative space, no borders, no logos, no lettering, no readable text. Each person has exactly two arms and two hands, natural credible hand positions. This is a real narrative scene rather than a UI screenshot or a portrait. Scene: A quiet university library study table by a tall window. The glasses-wearing student writes notes in an open notebook with their right hand; their left hand rests on an open textbook. A small stack of books and a desk lamp, sunlight suggested only through pencil shading. Medium wide view, calm attentive mood, show the learning environment as much as the student.
